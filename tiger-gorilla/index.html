Tiger Gorilla

Daniel Vaughan

A tiger-striped gorilla. A turbo-charged bottom. Twelve levels of jungle mischief.

A keyboard is needed to play. Click Play to load the game with sound.

At the title screen, press 2 for keyboard, then Space to start. Swing through the jungle, catch parrots and feed the babies.
