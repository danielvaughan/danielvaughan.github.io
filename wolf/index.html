WOLF

Daniel Vaughan

Join the WOLF Pack. Find your rhythm. Earn your place on the board.

A keyboard is needed to play. Click Play to load the game with sound.

Press Space to start and advance the opening screens. Alternate O and P steadily during a set, and tap Space when the TOP and BOTTOM lamps light.

O / P
Walk left / right
Alternate O and P
Pump during sets, sled, bike and ski; break away from chats
Space
Interact / start; hit the top and bottom beats during reps
A
Ask for more weight after a clean set
H
Pause / resume in game
B
View the board while paused; sneak off at the finisher card
M
Music on / off at title or in-game pause
Q
Quit to title while paused
